Zib

The friend who lives in Word Land and carries the lost letters home.
Everything here is drawn in the page — no images to load, any size, always sharp.

How he feels

Nine faces. His antennae are the tell: they glow when he speaks, droop when he's thinking, and sag when something didn't work.

Big and small

The same drawing at every size — a welcome screen, a header, a corner button.

Making a new player

This is the whole thing a child sees the first time, at real speed. Nothing on screen needs reading — Zib asks, they answer.

Everything to record

Tap any line to hear it and see Zib say it. The grey text is the filename — drop your recording in at exactly that path and Zib uses your voice instead.

Lines marked rising run straight into the child's name — record them as though the sentence hasn't finished. praise/nice-to-meet-you.mp3 then players/player1.mp3 becomes "Nice to meet you, Sarah."
